Privacy Policy

Stirring Minds Social (social.stirringminds.com) · Last updated: 12 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Stirring Minds Services Pvt Ltd (“Stirring Minds”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use Stirring Minds Social, our social media scheduling tool at social.stirringminds.com (the “Service”), including when you connect Facebook, Instagram, Threads or other social media accounts to it. It supplements our company-wide Privacy Policy; where the two differ for the Service, this policy applies.

1. Who we are

Stirring Minds Services Pvt Ltd, 2-A/3 Kundan Mansion, Asaf Ali Road, New Delhi 110002, India. Privacy contact: pranav@flashspace.ai.

2. What the Service does

The Service lets a signed-in user connect social media accounts that they own or manage, compose posts, preview them, schedule them for a chosen time, publish them to the connected accounts, and view basic performance statistics (such as reach, likes and comments) for the posts published through it. It is operated for Stirring Minds, its portfolio brands (for example FlashSpace) and the businesses and team members we work with.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account information. When you create a Service account we store your name, email address and a hashed password (or the identity returned by a sign-in provider), plus your settings and the posts, drafts, media and schedules you create.

3.2 Information from connected social media platforms. When you connect an account (for example a Facebook Page, an Instagram professional account or a Threads profile), the platform asks for your permission and then gives the Service:

  • an access token that lets the Service act on that account on your behalf, which we store encrypted and use only for the features described here;
  • basic profile information for the connected account: its platform ID, name or username, and profile picture;
  • for Facebook: the list of Pages you manage (pages_show_list), Page access tokens, and Page engagement and insight data (pages_read_engagement, read_insights); the ability to create and manage posts and comments on those Pages (pages_manage_posts, pages_manage_engagement); and the business assets needed to find Pages owned through a business portfolio (business_management);
  • for Instagram: the professional account linked to your Page, its media and basic information (instagram_basic), the ability to publish content (instagram_content_publish), to read and reply to comments on your posts (instagram_manage_comments) and to read insights for your account and posts (instagram_manage_insights);
  • for Threads: your profile (threads_basic), the ability to publish threads (threads_content_publish), manage replies (threads_manage_replies) and read insights (threads_manage_insights).

We only use these permissions to provide the Service to you. We do not read your private messages, your friends list or anyone else's data, and we never post, reply or change anything on your accounts except what you explicitly create or schedule in the Service.

3.3 Technical information. Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) and strictly necessary cookies used to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies or trackers.

4. How we use information

  • to connect your accounts and publish, schedule, edit or delete the posts you create;
  • to show you previews, statistics and comments for your posts;
  • to keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and fix problems;
  • to respond to your support requests.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use data obtained from Facebook, Instagram or Threads for advertising, profiling, training models or any purpose other than providing the Service to you.

5. Sharing

Data is shared only with: (a) the social media platforms you connect, to carry out the actions you request; (b) the hosting provider that runs our server; (c) authorities where the law requires it. The Service is self-hosted on infrastructure we control and does not pass your connected-account data to the makers of the underlying software.

6. Platform policies

Our use of information received from Meta platforms complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. Our use of information received from other platforms complies with their respective developer terms.

7. Storage, security and retention

Data is stored on servers we control, protected by TLS in transit, encrypted access tokens, access controls and regular updates. Connected-account data is kept only while the account stays connected. When you disconnect an account, its access token and cached profile and statistics data are deleted from our systems; posts you created in the Service remain in your Service account until you delete them or your account. Server logs are kept for up to 90 days.

8. Your rights and choices

  • You can disconnect any social media account at any time from the Service (see Data Deletion), or revoke the Service's access from the platform's own settings (for Facebook and Instagram: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations; for Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions).
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by emailing pranav@flashspace.ai. We answer within 30 days.

9. Children

The Service is for business use by adults and is not directed at anyone under 18.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be announced inside the Service.
