User Data Deletion

Stirring Minds Social (social.stirringminds.com) · Last updated: 12 October 2026

You can remove the data Stirring Minds Social holds about you and your connected Facebook, Instagram, Threads or other social media accounts at any time. Any of the three options below works; you do not need to do all of them.

Option 1 – Disconnect the account inside the Service (immediate)
  1. Sign in at social.stirringminds.com.
  2. In the left channel list, open the menu (⋯) next to the connected channel.
  3. Choose Disable channel or Delete channel. Deleting removes the account's access token and the cached profile, statistics and comment data from our systems straight away.
  4. To delete your whole Service account and everything in it, go to Settings and use the account deletion option, or email us (Option 3).
Option 2 – Revoke access from the platform
  • Facebook / Instagram: open Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find “Postiz Stirring Minds” and choose Remove. You can tick the option to delete posts, videos or events the app published, if any.
  • Threads: open Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions, find “Postiz Threads Stirring Minds” and remove it.
Revoking access invalidates the token we hold; we delete the associated data within 30 days of the revocation.
Option 3 – Email us

Send an email to pranav@flashspace.ai with the subject “Data deletion request” from the email address of your Service account, or with the name of the connected Page or profile. We will delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

Access tokens for the connected accounts, cached profile information (IDs, names, usernames, pictures), cached statistics and comments, and, on account deletion, your posts, drafts, media, settings and account record. Server logs that may contain your IP address are deleted automatically within 90 days. Where the law requires us to keep a record (for example invoices), we keep only that record.
