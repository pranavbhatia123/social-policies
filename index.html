Stirring Minds Social

A social media scheduling tool operated by Stirring Minds Services Pvt Ltd

Stirring Minds Social (social.stirringminds.com) is a self-hosted social media scheduling and publishing tool run by Stirring Minds Services Pvt Ltd (New Delhi, India) for its team, its portfolio brands such as FlashSpace, and the businesses it works with. It is built on the open-source Postiz platform.

With it, a user connects social media accounts they own or manage, such as Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts, Threads profiles and X accounts, and then writes, previews, schedules and publishes posts to those accounts from one place, and views basic performance statistics for those posts.

  • Terms of Service – the rules for using the tool.
